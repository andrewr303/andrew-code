# HypeStake Product Context

Default product context for Design Surgeon.

HypeStake is a creator-brand equity marketplace and AI operating system. The interface is fintech-adjacent, trust-sensitive, data-heavy, and workflow-rich. It supports:

- Creator discovery and matching.
- Creator Genome intelligence and CERS-like scoring.
- Campaign planning and creator shortlists.
- Deal structuring across equity, revenue-share, cash, and hybrid offers.
- Company Analysis, portfolio analysis, Shadow Cap Table, vesting, and scenario economics.
- Brand Hub and Creator Hub workflows.
- Admin ops, AI runs, queues, enrichment, cache, SEO, and valuation operations.

## Visual defaults

- Base: midnight navy / near-black shell.
- Accents: violet, cyan, and magenta used semantically and sparingly.
- Surfaces: solid panels, fine borders, compact density, readable contrast.
- Data UI: auditable tables, source/freshness/confidence labels, actionable charts.
- Typography: credible product sans for text, distinct numeric handling for metrics.

## Domain-specific checks

Creator discovery cards should expose fit, audience, engagement, price estimate, freshness, source, confidence, risk, and next action.

Creator Genome should not feel like black-box theater. Scores need evidence, dimensions, benchmarks, and confidence.

Deal Architect surfaces need equity percent, cash, rev-share, vesting, KPI triggers, deliverables, compliance caveats, and inspectable assumptions.

Company Analysis needs valuation, ARR/runway, scenarios, blocked metrics, confidence, source, freshness/cache state, and caveats.

Portfolio surfaces must distinguish paper value from cash or realized income.

Admin surfaces must expose run status, stale jobs, failed jobs, retry actions, cache state, logs, and audit trail.
